What are we measuring?

Give me a domain. I'll read the site and work out the brand, the product, who it competes with, and the topics buyers actually argue about. You get to correct all of it on the next screen.

The hint matters when a company sells many things. Without it you may end up measuring the wrong product line.

Fix what it got wrong

This is the highest-leverage screen in the app. Everything downstream inherits these decisions, and a bad competitor list quietly corrupts twelve weeks of data.

Competitors

Mark anything you own as a sibling. A brand you own is not a rival — but it is still worth tracking, because assistants routinely confuse a parent brand with its subsidiary, and that confusion is findable in the data.

Topics

Keep names short — two or three words. They become axis labels in the 3D views and long ones clip.

The query set

These get sent verbatim, every run, forever. Once you start collecting, changing them breaks week-over-week comparison — so read them now. Roughly 60% deliberately do not name you: that is where you find out whether you get surfaced at all.

Collect

Each run is one snapshot. Run it weekly and the timeline builds itself. Answers are stored in this browser as they arrive, so a closed tab costs you the run in progress, not the history.

Repeats are not optional at scale. These models are stochastic — ask the same question twice and you get different sources. One repeat is a spot check; three is a measurement. Start at one to sanity-check quality, then raise it.
